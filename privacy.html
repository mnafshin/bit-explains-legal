Privacy Policy

Bit Explains Uploader · Effective date: October 22, 2026

Bit Explains Uploader is a private tool used only by the owner of the Bit Explains channel to upload his own videos to his own TikTok account through the official TikTok API. It is not offered to the public.

What data is used

  • OAuth tokens issued by TikTok after the account owner authorizes the app, and the account's open ID.
  • Basic creator info returned by TikTok (username, nickname, allowed privacy options, maximum video length) to choose valid post settings.
  • The upload/post status of videos the tool itself sent.

How it is used and stored

Data is used only to upload and check the owner's own videos. Tokens are stored in a private file readable only by the owner on his own server. Nothing is sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or used to track anyone. No data about other TikTok users is collected.

Retention and deletion

Tokens are kept until access is revoked. Access can be revoked at any time in TikTok under Settings → Security → Manage app permissions; the token file is then deleted.
